# Assignment 0: Compute Account Setup

## Overview

The purpose of this assignment is to make sure everyone has accounts set up for the computing platforms we expect to use throughout the semester. These resources will become increasingly more important as the course progresses so please make sure that you register for each one soon and begin getting familiar with how they work sooner rather than later. Listed below are three of the primary cloud compute resources we will use. There will also be a course compute cluster available for which we will provide information later.

## 1. ACCESS / Delta

We will use **Delta**, a high-performance computing system operated by the National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA), through the **ACCESS** program. 

### Create your ACCESS account

1. Go to the ACCESS account registration page and create an **ACCESS ID**.
   1. [https://account.access-ci.org/register](https://account.access-ci.org/register)
2. Whenever possible, register using your **university account / institutional login**.
3. Use your **university email address** and make sure your institutional affiliation is listed correctly.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled out in your profile.
   1. ACCESS may not display an error for an incomplete profile, but missing information can prevent us from adding you to the course allocation.
5. Complete the email verification step sent to your university email.
6. Record your **ACCESS ID/username**.

If you already have an ACCESS or former XSEDE account, **do not create a second account**.

### Join the course project

You do **not** need to request your own ACCESS allocation or Delta project.

Instead, submit your **ACCESS ID** as part of this assignment. We will use your ACCESS ID to add you to the course's existing ACCESS project and grant you access to the appropriate Delta resources.

Once we add you to the project, ACCESS/NCSA will begin provisioning your account on Delta. This process may take several days.

### After you are added to Delta

Watch your university email for any account setup messages from ACCESS or NCSA and complete any requested setup steps.

You can also check the status of your resource accounts through the **My Projects** area of the ACCESS Allocations portal. Once provisioned, your NCSA/Delta username should appear with the project/resource information.

**If you are not seeing the addition of Delta to your account two weeks after the submission, please email the TA: Nolan Brady ([Nolan.Brady@colorado.edu](mailto:Nolan.Brady@colorado.edu)).**

## 2. Google Cloud

In addition to Delta, we will also be using **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** for some course activities. Each student is eligible to receive a **$50 Google Cloud Education credit** for this course.

### Request your Google Cloud credit

1. Open the course's **Google Cloud Student Coupon Retrieval Link**, posted in the Canvas announcement for this assignment.
2. Enter your name and your **University of Colorado Boulder email address** (`@colorado.edu`).
3. Google will send a confirmation email to that address. Follow the instructions in the email to verify your information.
4. After verification, Google will provide you with a **Google Cloud coupon code**.
5. Follow Google's instructions to redeem the coupon and associate the credit with your Google Cloud account.

Each student may request **only one coupon per unique email address**.

Please use your `@colorado.edu` address when requesting the coupon. Do **not** request multiple coupon codes or use another email domain.

### Important dates and credit information

- **Credit amount:** $50 USD
- **Coupon request and redemption deadline:** December 20, 2026
- **Credit valid through:** August 20, 2027
- **Eligible student email domain:** `@colorado.edu`
- **Limit:** One coupon per unique email address

Although the redemption deadline is December 20, please request and redeem your coupon **as soon as possible** so that your account is ready whenever you need additional compute resources.

**Do not activate the standard Google Cloud Free Trial or enter a personal credit card unless specifically instructed to do so for a later assignment.** For this course, use the education coupon provided through the link on Canvas.

For this assignment, record the `@colorado.edu` email address you used to request the coupon and the current status of your credit.

## 3. Chameleon Cloud

Finally, we will also be using **Chameleon Cloud**, an NSF-supported experimental cloud computing platform.

Chameleon uses federated authentication, so you do not need to create a separate Chameleon password.

### Create your Chameleon account

1. Go to the Chameleon Cloud website and select **Log In**.
   1. [https://chameleoncloud.org/](https://chameleoncloud.org/)
2. Choose **Sign in via federated identity**.
3. Use your university/institutional credentials when possible.
4. Complete the initial account setup and accept the Chameleon terms of use.
5. The email you use with the institution login will serve as your Chameleon username going forward.



### Request access to the course allocation

After signing in to Chameleon, open the **course project join link**, posted in the Canvas announcement for this assignment, and submit the request to join the project.

The request will then be sent to the course project managers for approval. You do not need to create your own Chameleon project.
